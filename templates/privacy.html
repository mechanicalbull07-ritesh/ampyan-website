Privacy

Privacy Policy

AMPYAN respects your privacy and is committed to handling your information responsibly.

Last Updated: 2026

App data, providers and deletion

AMPYAN processes account identity, vehicle and service records, document and checklist inputs, submitted vehicle-problem text and content you choose to publish to provide its features. Optional profile details include phone number, age, gender and city. Processing includes Render hosting, Google sign-in, consent-controlled native Firebase Analytics and the speech recognition service selected on your Android device, as used by the relevant feature. Backend operational and performance logging is separate from optional native Analytics. Stopping Analytics collection does not erase data already transmitted to the provider. Speech-to-text supplies recognized text to the app; the reviewed speech-to-text path does not save a raw-audio recording. Recognition may involve remote processing under the provider’s terms; on-device-only processing is not guaranteed. Typed input remains available. Public content you submit can be visible to other users. Request account deletion at https://ampyan.com/account-deletion. The workflow requires identity verification and manual processing. We do not offer recovery of accounts after completed deletion. Returning users must register a new account; this does not restore their previous account data. Any necessary lawful retention exception must be limited to its stated purpose and does not authorize account recovery. Individual service records can be deleted in Garage without deleting the account. Processor records, operational logs, backups and offline local copies require separate handling; account deletion does not guarantee instantaneous erasure of every copy. For privacy inquiries, contact hiampyan@gmail.com.

Paid-service costs entered in Garage form service purchase history. Google speech provider information: Google Privacy Policy. Other Android recognition services may have different terms.

AMPYAN collects only the information needed to operate your account, deliver vehicle-related features and improve the product experience.

Information We Collect

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Vehicle information such as brand, model, year and fuel type
  • Community posts, comments and app usage signals

Google Login

AMPYAN may allow sign-in with Google. We only receive basic profile information such as your email address and do not access passwords, Drive, contacts or unrelated private Google data.

How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the platform and your account
  • To provide vehicle intelligence insights and garage features
  • To improve diagnostic quality and product accuracy
  • To support community and trust systems

Data Protection

We implement reasonable security measures to help protect user data and reduce unauthorized access risk. No platform can promise absolute security, but AMPYAN is designed to handle information with care.
