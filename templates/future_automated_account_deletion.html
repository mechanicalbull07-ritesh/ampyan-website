What happens

Enter your account email and confirm your request. If the account can be located, deletion-specific instructions are queued for the AMPYAN administrator to send only to the email stored on that account. Provider acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery. Enter the request reference and code below. The AMPYAN administrator processes verified requests. Sending a request or verifying it does not mean deletion is complete.

Deletion removes identifiable account/authentication/profile data, vehicles, configuration, service records, mileage/health data, Diagnosis and private interactions. Public discussion may remain only without the deleted user's identity and personal contribution content; other users' replies are preserved.

Logging out or uninstalling is not account deletion. Offline local device copies cannot be erased remotely; the App clears the deleted owner's records when it next receives completion. Clear local App data if you no longer use the App.

Some information may be retained only where required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or backup integrity. No fixed retention duration is established here. Any applicable exception must be reviewed and disclosed as part of processing; we do not retain all personal data by default. Backup restores must reapply completed deletion decisions.

Verification and completion routing data is encrypted and used only while needed for processing or notification recovery. Check your receipt status in the App. Completion email acceptance by the mail provider is not a guarantee of inbox delivery. Processing/provider failures remain pending for operator recovery; no completion is claimed until required cleanup is verified.
